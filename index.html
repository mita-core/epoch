01 / Why come

You will be
typing, not
taking notes

Most of the programme is a room with tables, laptops open, and someone who has shipped the thing standing behind your chair. The talks are there to set up the workshops, not the other way round. Nobody is selling anything from a stand.

02 / The days

The same shape on Thursday and Friday. Saturday runs the morning only and ends at 2pm.

How a day
at epoch runs

  1. ↘ 01

    Doors at eight

    Coffee and the day's board from 8am. Sessions are first come; the board tells you what still has seats and what filled up overnight.

  2. ↘ 02

    Morning talks

    Two talks before 11am, forty minutes each. Both set up a workshop you can walk straight into afterwards, in the room next door.

  3. ↘ 03

    Workshop block

    Three hours at a table with your own laptop. You leave the block with something that runs, or with a clear account of why it doesn't.

  4. ↘ 04

    Lunch outside

    Ninety minutes on the pier, nothing on the schedule. This is the hour people tell us about afterwards, so we stopped programming over it.

  5. ↘ 05

    Office hours

    Speakers sit at numbered tables from 3pm. Bring the problem you actually have. Six slots per table, fifteen minutes each, booked on the day.

  6. ↘ 06

    Demos, then dinner

    Live demos from 5:30pm — running code, twenty minutes, questions from the floor. Dinner in the same room after, no seating plan.
